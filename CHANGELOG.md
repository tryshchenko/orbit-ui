# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-03

Initial release.

- `@orbit/tokens`: primitive scales, 4 themes, materials, density, motion, contrast utilities, generated CSS/JSON, and a contrast test suite covering glass over real backdrops.
- `@orbit/icons`: Lucide re-export plus Orbit logo and issue-type glyphs.
- `@orbit/ui`: foundation, Aero and product components (see docs/components.md).
- Storybook design workspace with theme, density and transparency toolbars and per-story interaction + axe tests.
- Orbit Projects demo with Playwright e2e, axe, visual regression and web-vitals tests.
